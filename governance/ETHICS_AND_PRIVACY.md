# Ethics and Privacy Statement

The C-SMDT project is committed to the highest standards of bioethics and data privacy. Because this is an *in silico* modeling pipeline, the data generation workflows strictly enforce non-identifiable parameters configurations.

## 1. Compliance with Human Subjects Privacy (HIPAA)
* **Complete Anonymization**: All geometry points, anatomical boundaries, and biometric time-series datasets used to derive these simulations are **completely synthetic and anonymized** in strict accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Safe Harbor framework.
* **Exclusion of PHI**: No Protected Health Information (PHI), raw DICOM image headers, or individual astronaut tracking parameters are permitted within the public codebase. 

## 2. Institutional Review Board (IRB) Status
The computational model workflows utilize exclusively open-access, aggregate, de-identified control data pools extracted from public aerospace medicine registries. Consequently, this study does not constitute direct human subjects research and is classified as **IRB Exempt** under standard institutional bioethics evaluation checklists.
